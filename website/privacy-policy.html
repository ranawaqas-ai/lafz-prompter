Lafz Prompter Features

Privacy Policy

Last updated in November 2026

Data collection

Lafz Prompter does not collect usage data and does not send anything to its developer. The app contains neither analytics nor telemetry, and it does not require an account.

Scripts and settings

Your settings are stored in the preferences of the app on your Mac. Your scripts are stored in a file on your Mac, ~/Library/Application Support/Lafz Prompter/scripts.json.

Microphone for voice activation

Voice activation needs access to the microphone. The app uses the microphone only while voice activation is listening, which starts after you press Play. Audio is measured for loudness on your Mac in real time and is not recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Call Assist

Call Assist is an experimental feature. It is off until you start it, and it needs an API key from Anthropic or OpenAI that you enter yourself.

  • While it runs, it transcribes your microphone and your Mac's system audio on your Mac, using the speech recognition built into macOS. The audio is never saved.
  • When it detects a question, or when you ask it for an answer, it sends the recent transcript, your instructions and your knowledge base files to the provider you chose, using your API key. That provider's privacy policy applies to what it receives.
  • If you turn on "Include a screenshot with each answer", a screenshot of the display you are working on is sent with each answer. Solve the screen (⌃⌥S) sends a screenshot even when that setting is off. The app keeps only the newest screenshot, in memory.
  • Your API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • If History is on (it is by default), questions and answers are saved in a file on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Lafz Prompter/. You can turn History off and delete it in Settings.

Please check that the people on a call agree to being transcribed before you use Call Assist.

Network access

The app connects to the network for two things. Once a day it checks for a new version by downloading a small file from lafz.waqasrana.space, and when you install an update it downloads that version from GitHub. The request carries the app's version and, like any web request, your IP address. It sends no other information, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which gives the developer no record of who checked. You can turn the check off under Settings › Behavior. The second is Call Assist, which connects only to the provider you chose (api.anthropic.com or api.openai.com). The first time Call Assist runs, macOS may download its speech recognition model from Apple.

Permissions

  • Microphone, for voice activation and for your side of a call in Call Assist.
  • System audio recording, for the other side of a call in Call Assist.
  • Screen recording, optional, only for Call Assist answers with a screenshot.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those services see your IP address as part of serving the page. The site itself does not set cookies or use analytics.
